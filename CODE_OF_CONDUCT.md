# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the cloud-pulse community harassment-free and welcoming, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, publication of private information without permission, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), identifying that the report concerns conduct. Maintainers may clarify expectations, issue warnings, remove content, or restrict participation proportionately. Retaliation against reporters is not tolerated.
